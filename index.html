ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

A Legacy of Knowledge & Values

BELL AND BENNETT located at Addanki in Prakasam District, A.P. is an English Medium Co-educational School recognized by Government of A.P. founded in 1993 with 28 students, 2 staff on roll and has consistently grown into a big family of around 1000 students and a good number of teaching and non-teaching staff as on today. BELL AND BENNETT SCHOOL imparts international standards by adopting the latest methodologies, implementing innovative measures and ideas.

From its inception onwards the school was successful in retaining as an educational institution with a difference by way of offering a host of features and training programs aimed at improving the academic, social, intellectual and behavioural skills of students.

The school offers IIT Foundation courses from classes III onwards. With a view to making education student friendly and interesting to the first time school goers, the school has designed Smart Kindergarten with latest technologies and innovative. There is a comprehensive academic schedule for LKG and UKG which reduces the burden on children, teachers and parents and makes learning a stimulating and rewarding experience.

Our Journey

1993
Started as "Little Angels" with 28 kindergarten students by Mr. R.J. Vijaya Kumar and Mrs. Elizabeth Vijaya Kumar.
1998
Renamed Bell & Bennett after US missionaries Bro. Russel G Bell and Bro. Gary L Bennett.
2003-04
Earned recognition for classes I to VII from the government of Andhra Pradesh.
2010-11
Achieved recognition up to X class, completing our secondary school milestone.

Our Vision

BELL AND BENNETT HIGH SCHOOL, aims to build a strong foundation by inculcating deep rooted cultural and social values among its students. The school trains them to be god fearing individuals with versatile personalities who will one day serve the nation with their unique skills, passionate ideas and humble nature.

Our Mission

BELL AND BENNETT School is committed to impart education having global standards with innovative practices. We aim to provide quality education through discipline and academic brilliance, enabling the students to achieve success in life with human excellence and human values.

Core Values

Integrity

We believe in creating a culture of integrity, where we build strong characters and to help them act ethically and consistently.

Acceptance

Our mission is to confront ignorance with knowledge, prejudice with informed approval and resentment with empathy.

Global consciousness

We cultivate universal awareness among our students to have a greater understanding and appreciation of other languages, cultures, history, customs, and religions.

World-Class Facilities

Hostel Life

  • Separate dormitories for junior and senior boys and girls.
  • AC and water heating system available.
  • Special tutors to take care of academic progress.
  • Wardens in-charge ensuring clean, pleasant, and friendly environment.
  • Nutritious South Indian cuisine (Veg & Non-Veg) changing with seasons.
  • Medical attention cared by wardens with parental consultation.

Transportation

The school operates a fleet of 3 buses in a radius of 20 kms. The students are to commute only by the authorized school vehicles so as to ensure safety and security of our children. Private conveyance is allowed only with prior permission of school administration.

Toy Room

The activity centre for the tiny tots is lovingly addressed as the ‘Toy Room’. It is a well-decorated, vibrant and colourful place giving endless opportunities to students to explore, create, design and discover the fantastic world outside within its secure and affectionate walls.

"Knowledge, Peace & Service."

Dear well wisher, Today, in a world where society is increasingly fragmented and polarized, our school remains a community where honesty and integrity matter. We are a school community of high expectations, academic and otherwise. At the same time, we are devoted to helping students develop intellectual, artistic and personal interests contributing to holistic growth of each individual. Our motto “KNOWLEDGE, PEACE and SERVICE” speaks volumes about the spirit of the school and inspires the budding students.

This educational institution, which is 34 years old today, as in the case of every other institution, had a small and humble beginning. It had its ups and downs during its three decades of existence. In spite of its glorious feat as one of the best educational institutions in A.P., it never commercializes education but continues to remain an institution of service, catering to the educational needs of children. BELL AND BENNETT has tried its best to excel in all departments of its students’ growth. The specialty of this institute is that it has made quality education accessible to all, high and low, rich and poor, thanks to the highly motivated staff and the availability of quality infrastructure.

During these past 33 years, the institution has created an environment and ambience that fosters learning, as it provides the students with opportunities to explore and grow, through situations that challenge them to identify their strengths and reinforce them. In this regard, the words of the great scientist Albert Einstein are memorable when he said, “I don’t teach my children. I create conditions for them to learn.”

Central to our efforts to build an intellectual community is our faculty. Intensely devoted to the success of their students, the faculty serves as teachers, coaches, advisors, mentors and friends. In every area of school life, the faculty seeks to challenge and inspire students to reach new levels of enquiry and understanding. While we establish the foundation of your child’s learning and ascertain his/her thorough development to enhance his/her present, we also undertake to prepare him/her for the highly competitive arena of further studies for his/her bright future.

I welcome parents and students to be a part of the BELL AND BENNETT family to share common ideals and aspirations as we build a challenging and enriching educational experience for our students. It may be stated here that B & B has been rated as one of the most looked-up-to-institutions and is going through an unprecedented era of development with your sincere and devoted support and blessings. I would like to thank every one of you for your priceless contribution. Joint efforts of the management, faculty and parents in this noble task are always recognised and appreciated. Together we can transform the learners, giving them the wings of knowledge, responsible usage of technology along with the strength of wisdom to make the world a better place.

With lots of respect and love,

Admissions Open for 2026–27

Parents seeking admissions for LKG, the age should be 3 years and 6 months plus during the year of joining. Online Registration for the admission can be done or else can contact the school office. Mere online registration does not ensure the admission. Admission to the school will be decided after the interaction session. Parents must accompany their ward at the time of interaction session. Students seeking admission other than LKG, UKG and Class I, will have to produce Transfer Certificate, Conduct Certificate and Mark List from the school last attended.
